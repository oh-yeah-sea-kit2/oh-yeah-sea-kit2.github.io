Collection of Usage Data (In-App Behavior Log)

To help us improve the quality of the app, we send anonymous usage data to our own server (which we operate on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, Inc.).

Examples of information we send:

  • Screens viewed and screen transitions (which screens were viewed, and in what order)
  • Button taps and in-game interaction events (e.g., starting a game, achieving bingo, whether a photo was successfully taken)
  • The general category of an error that occurred (not the raw error message or stack trace — only a classification such as "network error" or "rendering error")
  • OS type and major version, app build number, and whether the simplified-hiragana display mode is enabled, and similar device/setting information needed for improving the app

Information we do not send:

  • Name, email address, date of birth, or age
  • Raw location data (nearby-spot search only reports whether a result was found, never raw coordinates)
  • Photos or drawings themselves
  • The literal text you type, such as card titles or report descriptions
  • The literal text of error messages or stack traces (these remain on your device only and are never transmitted)
  • Any account ID or device-specific identifier (the identifier used for measurement is a random, anonymous ID that is technically kept fully separate from your account or device identity)

About identifiers: Usage data is associated only with the anonymous random ID described above (issued per app session). This ID cannot be technically linked to your login information or device identity, and is never shared with third parties.

About your IP address: Your device's source IP address is referenced only temporarily, and solely to prevent abusive high-volume submissions (rate limiting). It is never stored or analyzed as part of usage data.

Retention period: Individual usage records (raw logs) are automatically deleted after a maximum of 92 days. Statistical data that summarizes many records (such as screen-transition trends or error rates) may be retained for a longer period to support ongoing service improvement. No individual can be identified from this statistical data.

Opting out: Sending of usage data is enabled by default, but a parent or guardian can turn it off at any time from the parental menu inside Settings. Once turned off, no further data is sent. Children are never asked to consent to this themselves.

Account Linking and Cloud Save (Optional Features)

All core features of this app can be used without creating an account. Optionally, you can sign in with a Google account or Apple ID from the "Account Linking" screen located behind the guardian verification screen. When you link an account, we obtain only your account identifier, email address, and the type of sign-in provider, and we use them solely to save and restore your original bingo cards in the cloud and to transfer them when you switch devices.

  • Only the card composition data (such as titles and cell texts) is stored in the cloud. Photos taken on your device are never uploaded to or published through the cloud.
  • You can request to publish your cards to the shared gallery (subject to review). Only the card content itself is published; your name, email address, or other personal details are never shown to other users.
  • Authentication credentials (tokens) are stored only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Deleting Your Account and Cloud Data

You can delete your account and cloud-saved data at any time from within the app (Adults Menu → Account Linking). Upon deletion, your linked account information (including your email address), cloud-saved cards, and published cards are removed from our servers and can no longer be accessed. Cards, play history, and photos stored on your device are not deleted and remain available.
