1. How the Public Gallery Works

All another user can do is view a card, import it to their own device to play with it, report it to us (section 6), and hide it so that it no longer appears on their own screen.

3. Photos Are Never Published (Drawings Are)

A card containing photo squares can be published, but what appears to other people is only the text entered on that square. The photo itself is never visible to anyone else.
  • Photo image data is not sent to our server, even when you submit the card for publication.
  • This is not merely an operational rule but a structural restriction. The published data structure has no field that can reference a photo.
  • A square that uses a photo must have text entered on it before the card can be published, because otherwise it would appear blank to other people.

3-2. How Drawings Are Handled When Published

Drawings you create inside the App can be published. However, only images that a human reviewer has looked at and approved are ever made public.
  • Until an image passes review, no one else can obtain it — not even someone who knows its URL. The same applies to images that were rejected, suspended, or withdrawn.
  • Withdrawing a card stops delivery of its images immediately.
  • If you delete the card, or delete your account, we delete the image data from our storage.
  • You cannot publish, as your own card, an image belonging to someone else that came with a card you imported from the Public Gallery.
Please check what you draw. Do not write identifying information — your name, address or school — into the picture. Our review cannot catch everything. If you are unsure, do not publish it.

5. Publication Is Subject to Review

Cards submitted for publication are checked in the following order.

  1. Automated filter: a mechanical check for inappropriate wording, strings that look like contact details or URLs, text that looks like a personal name, address, or school name, length limits, and so on.
  2. Human review: our reviewer checks the content. A card is published only if it is approved in this review.
  • A card may be rejected as a result of the review. A rejected card is not published, but it remains on your device and in the cloud as a private card. You may edit it and submit it again.
  • Review takes some time. You can check the status of your submission in the App.
  • In addition to violations of these Terms, we may refuse publication or suspend a published card without stating an individual reason where we judge it necessary to keep the Public Gallery safe.

  • If our review finds a problem, we stop publication of the card and take the measures in section 9 where appropriate. If it finds no problem, the card simply stays published. A card whose publication we have stopped may be published again if a further review finds no problem.
  • The contents of a report are not disclosed to the author of the reported card, and we do not publish information that identifies the reporter.
  • Each person may report a given card once.
  • Reports that are untrue, or repeated reports targeting a particular card, may be treated as abuse of the reporting function and may lead to the measures in section 9.
