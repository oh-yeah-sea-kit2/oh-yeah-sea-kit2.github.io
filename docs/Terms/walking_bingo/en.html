  4. Discriminatory content: content that demeans or excludes people on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, illness, religion, occupation, or similar grounds.

  6. Content that infringes the rights of others: using another person's copyrighted work, characters, trademarks, or likeness without the rights holder's permission.
  7. URLs and external links: web addresses, QR code content, or any other string that directs readers to an external service.
  8. Advertising, solicitation, or commercial content, and conduct that interferes with the operation of the Public Gallery, such as posting the same content in bulk.

Such content is screened out by an automated filter at the time of submission and by our review. However, the filter and the review are not perfect. The person submitting the card (and the guardian described in section 4) is responsible for ensuring that the content meets these standards.

3. Photos and Drawings Cannot Be Published

Only preset items and text can appear in the Public Gallery. A card that contains a square using a photo or a drawing cannot be published.
  • A card containing even one photo or drawing square is rejected at the point of submission.
  • This is not merely an operational rule but a structural restriction. The published data structure has no field that can reference an image, and there is no mechanism that serves images through the Public Gallery path.
  • Photos and drawings are used only on your own device and can never reach the Public Gallery.

If you want to publish a card that uses photos or drawings, replace those squares with text or preset items before submitting it.

  • Submitting a card for publication requires a linked Google account or Apple ID. Publication cannot be requested from an unlinked (anonymous) state.
  • Guardian approval does not guarantee publication. Even after approval, a card is published only if it passes our review (section 5).

5. Publication Is Subject to Review

Cards submitted for publication are checked in the following order.

  1. Automated filter: a mechanical check for inappropriate wording, strings that look like contact details or URLs, text that looks like a personal name, address, or school name, length limits, and so on.
  2. Human review: our reviewer checks the content. A card is published only if it is approved in this review.
  • A card may be rejected as a result of the review. A rejected card is not published, but it remains on your device and in the cloud as a private card. You may edit it and submit it again.
  • Review takes some time. You can check the status of your submission in the App.
  • In addition to violations of these Terms, we may refuse publication or suspend a published card without stating an individual reason where we judge it necessary to keep the Public Gallery safe.

6. Reporting

If you find problematic content on a published card, please report it.

  • Report from within the App: use "つうほうする" (Report) on the card detail screen in the Public Gallery, choose a category (inappropriate content / personal information is written here / an attempt to exchange contact details / it is scary or upsetting / other), and add a description of up to 200 characters if you wish. The report action sits behind the "おとなのかくにん" guardian gate. Users without a linked account can also report.
  • Contact us by email: for matters that do not fit the in-app report flow — rights-infringement notices, legal requests, takedown requests from third parties — please contact info+walking_bingo@oysk2.com, including information that identifies the card, such as its title.

How we handle reports:

  • A single report in the "personal information is written here" or "attempt to exchange contact details" category immediately makes the card unavailable to the public. The system stops publication at once without waiting for a person, and we then review the content.
  • For the other categories, a card is automatically made unavailable once it has received 3 reports, and we then review it.
  • If our review finds no problem, we may restore publication. If we find a problem, the card stays unpublished and we take the measures in section 9 where appropriate.
  • The contents of a report are not disclosed to the author of the reported card, and we do not publish information that identifies the reporter.
  • Each person may report a given card once.
  • Reports that are untrue, or repeated reports targeting a particular card, may be treated as abuse of the reporting function and may lead to the measures in section 9.
